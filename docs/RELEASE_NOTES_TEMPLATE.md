Free, open source, no account required. VST3 and AU, macOS (universal) and
Windows.

**Install:** unzip, drag into your plugin folder, restart your DAW.
Full instructions, including the macOS Gatekeeper step, are in `INSTALL.txt`
inside the download and in [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md).

**Heads up:** this plugin has around 128 ms of latency at default settings. Your
DAW compensates for it on playback; it is not for monitoring a live take
through. Record dry, then reach for it.

Every binary here was built and validated by GitHub Actions from the tagged
commit -- pluginval at strictness 10, `auval`, and the full test suite.
